# Punctuation Rules

Rules for each punctuation mark, with the house dash ban applied.

## Commas, Colons and Semicolons

- Use the serial comma: "zones, regions, and multi-regions".
- Put a comma after an introductory word or phrase. Put a comma before a conjunction that joins two independent clauses, unless both are very short.
- Put a comma before a nonrestrictive "which". Generally omit a comma before "because".
- Text before a colon must be a complete sentence: "The fields are defined as follows:". Start the text after a colon in lowercase.
- Avoid semicolons. Use them to join closely related clauses, before a conjunctive adverb, or between complex list items.

## Dashes and Hyphens

- **The house rule bans a dash used as a pause.** Google allows an unspaced em dash. This repository does not.
- Do not use an en dash. Use a hyphen or "to" for a range: "8-20 files" or "from 8 to 20 files".
- Separate a term from its description with a colon, a period, or a description list. Never use a spaced dash.
- Most prefixes take no hyphen (metadata). Hyphenate after "self-" and "cross-", before a capital or a number, and to avoid confusion.
- Hyphenate a compound modifier before a noun ("64-bit system"). Do not hyphenate "-ly" adverbs. Do not chain more than two words.

## Parentheses, Quotes and Periods

- Do not put important information in parentheses, because readers skip it. Keep a parenthetical brief.
- A period goes inside parentheses only when a full sentence stands inside them.
- Use straight double quotes and straight apostrophes. Commas and periods go inside the quotes, except after a literal code string.
- Use quotation marks for a shorter work title and a direct quote. Use italics for a full-length work.
- End a complete sentence with a period. Do not end a heading with a period. Do not put a period right after a URL.
- Avoid exclamation points in technical text.

## Ellipses and Slashes

- Avoid ellipses. Use three periods, never the ellipsis character, and omit text only from the middle of a quotation.
- Write the label `Save`, not `Save ...`, in UI references.
- Avoid slashes outside code. Write "or" or "and". Write "view and edit", not "view and/or edit".
- Do not write dates or fractions with slashes. Spell out "care of", not "c/o".
