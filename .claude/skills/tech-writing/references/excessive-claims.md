# Avoid Excessive Claims

Make product claims factual, narrow, and easy to verify.

Describe measured behavior and design choices. Do not promise results that evidence cannot prove.

## Support Claims With Evidence

- Avoid superlatives and absolutes such as best, fastest, never, or always.
- Cite a source for performance, cost, or resource-use figures.
- Limit a comparison to the tested conditions and named workload.
- Describe how a security feature contributes to security. Do not claim that it prevents every attack.
- Do not use words that promise certainty unless you can control and prove the result.
- Prefer specific, verifiable facts that are likely to stay accurate during the document's useful life.

## Examples

State that a design distributes data and computation in memory across a cluster, then report a measured speed result for that case. Do not say that the product is faster than a named competitor without scoped evidence.

State that a feature contributes to reducing phishing related account takeovers. Do not promise that the feature prevents them.

Source: [Google guidance on excessive claims](https://developers.google.com/style/excessive-claims).
