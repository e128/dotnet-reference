# Form Possessives

Form apostrophes by grammatical number, and avoid possessive product names and code identifiers.

Use possessives only when they make ownership or association clear. Rephrase a sentence when a possessive sounds awkward.

## Form the Possessive

- Add apostrophe plus s to a singular noun, including a noun that ends in s.
- Add an apostrophe after the s in a plural noun that ends in s.
- Add apostrophe plus s to a plural noun that does not end in s.
- Do not add an apostrophe to make a noun plural.
- Rewrite nested or awkward possessives with an of phrase or a clear modifier.

## Product Names and Code

- Use a product or feature name as a modifier, or use an of phrase, rather than making the name possessive for its function or performance.
- Use a company name's possessive form when the company owns something, unless the name is part of a trademarked product name.
- Do not make a code identifier possessive. Make the related noun possessive or describe what the code returns.

Source: [Google guidance on possessives](https://developers.google.com/style/possessives).
