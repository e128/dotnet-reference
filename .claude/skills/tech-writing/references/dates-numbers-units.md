# Dates, Numbers, and Units

Rules for dates, times, numbers, units, math and phone numbers.

## Dates and Times

- Spell out a full date with a four-digit year: "January 19, 2017". Month and year take no comma: "January 2017".
- Put a comma after the year in mid-sentence: "The January 19, 2017, release".
- Spell out the day of week when you use it: "Tuesday, April 27, 2021". Put the date before the time.
- Write a numeric-only date in ISO 8601: "2017-04-15". In examples, use a day greater than 12.
- Use a 12-hour clock with a space and capital AM or PM: "3 PM", not "15:00" or "3:00 PM". Omit minutes on a round hour.
- Avoid time zones. When needed, name the region and the UTC offset: "US Pacific Standard Time (UTC-8)".
- Avoid seasons. Use months or quarters.
- **Every timestamp is UTC ISO 8601**, such as `2026-05-05T22:00:00Z`.

## Numbers

- Spell out zero through nine and all ordinals. Use numerals for 10 and up.
- Always use numerals for technical quantities and versions: "6 queries per second", "128 bits", "version 3".
- Reword a sentence so it does not start with a numeral. If it must, spell the number out.
- Prefer decimals to fractions. Write a percentage as a numeral and `%` with no space.
- Write a range with a hyphen and no spaces: "2012-2016". Write dimensions as "192x192".
- Write US dollars as `$10,000`.

## Units and Math

- Put a nonbreaking space between a number and its unit: "64 GB". Currency, percent and degree signs take no space.
- Repeat the unit in a range and use "to": "-40 °C to 85 °C".
- Write rates with "per", not a slash: "requests per day".
- Match the byte system to the technology: decimal (kB, MB, GB) or binary (KiB, MiB, GiB).
- Use real math symbols, not keyboard stand-ins. Italicize variables, not operators. Use `<sup>` and `<sub>`, not `^`.
- Show complex equations as an image or with a math tool, with alt text.

## Phone Numbers

- Use only the reserved example range 800-555-0100 through 800-555-0199. Never use a real number.
- International format puts a plus sign directly before the country code: "+1-415-555-0132".
- Spell out "extension": "415-555-0132, extension 987".
