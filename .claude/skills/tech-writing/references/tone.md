# Use a Clear Tone

Use a direct, respectful tone that helps readers find and use the information.

Write in a natural, knowledgeable voice. Keep the reader's task ahead of personality, humor, or formality.

## Keep the Tone Clear

- Use plain words and explain technical terms when needed.
- Be polite through clear wording. Do not add please to routine instructions.
- Remove filler such as “please note” and “at this time.”
- Avoid slang, clichés, buzzwords, pop culture, jokes, and exclamation marks.
- Do not demean a group or invite action with “let's.”
- Do not call a procedure simple, easy, or quick. State the steps and requirements.
- Use transitions when they clarify the relationship between ideas.
- Do not start consecutive sentences with the same words, such as "You can" or "To".
- Read uncertain passages aloud or ask a colleague to review them.

## Choose Useful Wording

Prefer a factual description of what an API returns over exaggerated praise or a stiff passive description of data collection.

Prefer a direct instruction to call a method over a joke or a long explanation of the same action.

Source: [Google guidance on tone](https://developers.google.com/style/tone).
